About

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in mathematics at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Dori Bejleri.

Before coming to Maryland, I completed a B.Sc. in Mathematics and a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering at Boğaziçi University in Istanbul.

Here is my CV (last updated November 2026). My talks, teaching, and thesis and lecture notes are on separate pages.

Research

  • Number theory
  • Arithmetic geometry
  • Formalization

I am interested in number theory, arithmetic geometry, and formalization. My current project is on higher-genus motivic height zeta functions for weighted projective stacks. I am also interested in formal theorem proving and in benchmarking automated theorem provers.

  2. 2.
    MathAdv: What Theorem Provers Know, Reason, Formalize, and Generalize with Jiaxin Yuan, Connor Martinez Lockhart, and 11 others. Submitted, August 2026.
    arXiv:2608.25449 Code and data BibTeX
    Abstract

    Formal theorem proving enables machine-verifiable evaluation of mathematical reasoning, yet existing benchmarks often emphasize aggregate proof accuracy, concentrate on a narrow range of mathematics, and provide limited evidence of robustness to equivalent reformulations. We introduce MathAdv, a diagnostic benchmark spanning 13 domains across undergraduate- and graduate-level mathematics.

    All authors

    Co-authors: Jiaxin Yuan, Connor Martinez Lockhart, Xiaoyu Liu, Jiaqi Wang, Chenghao Deng, Xiayimei Han, Vlassis Mastrantonis, Dmitrii Gudin, Shaopeng Zhu, Abdirisak Mohamed, Jiewen Lang, Zezheng Song, and Furong Huang.

  3. 1.
    An Alexandrov Topology for Maximal Cohen–Macaulay Modules with Mert Akdenizli, Baran Çetin, and Özgür Esentepe. Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra 228 (2024), no. 1.
    Journal arXiv:2210.03532 BibTeX
    Abstract

    Using the theory of cohomology annihilators, we define a family of topologies on the set of isomorphism classes of maximal Cohen–Macaulay modules over a Gorenstein ring. We study compactness of these topologies.
