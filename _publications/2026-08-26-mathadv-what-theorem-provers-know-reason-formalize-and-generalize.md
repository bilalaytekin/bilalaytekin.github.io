Formal theorem proving enables machine-verifiable evaluation of mathematical reasoning, yet existing benchmarks often emphasize aggregate proof accuracy, concentrate on a narrow range of mathematics, and provide limited evidence of robustness to equivalent reformulations. We introduce MathAdv, a diagnostic benchmark spanning 13 domains across undergraduate- and graduate-level mathematics. The dataset and evaluation scripts are available at [this https URL](https://github.com/margotyjx/MathAdv.git). (With Jiaxin Yuan, Connor Martinez Lockhart, Xiaoyu Liu, Jiaqi Wang, Chenghao Deng, Xiayimei Han, Vlassis Mastrantonis, Dmitrii Gudin, Shaopeng Zhu, Abdirisak Mohamed, Jiewen Lang, Zezheng Song, Furong Huang.)

See paper [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25449).
